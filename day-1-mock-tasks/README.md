# Day 1: Mock Tasks

Pick **one** of these tasks if you don't have a work task you can bring. You'll carry it through all of Day 1, and on Day 2 you'll save it as a workflow.

Each task meets the joining-note test:
- it recurs at least weekly
- it takes 30 minutes or more
- its output can be checked

| Task | Who asks for it | How often | Time it takes by hand | Files |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| [1. Service desk weekly summary](task-1-service-desk-summary/) | Operations manager | Weekly, Monday | About 60 minutes | Ticket export (CSV), last week's summary |
| [2. Customer return replies](task-2-customer-return-replies/) | Service desk lead | Daily | About 10 minutes per email, 45 to 60 minutes a day | Five customer emails, returns policy |
| [3. Delivery exceptions report](task-3-delivery-exceptions/) | Purchasing lead | Weekly, Monday | About 45 minutes | Delivery log (CSV) |
| [4. Staff huddle digest](task-4-staff-huddle-digest/) | Warehouse supervisor | Weekly, Monday | About 30 minutes | Three internal notices |

Each folder's README has the request in the words of the person who asks for it. When you reach Lesson 3 (Define), you write the success criteria yourself.

Fictional sample for workshop use. Quillmoor Supply, its people, customers, suppliers and figures are invented.

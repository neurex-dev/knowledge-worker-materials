# Quillmoor Supply: Returns and Refunds Policy

Version 4. Effective 1 October 2026. Replaces version 3.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers parts, consumables and small appliances bought from Quillmoor Supply by business customers. Large equipment sold under a separate sales contract is covered by that contract.

## 2. Return windows

| Condition of the item | Return window, counted from the delivery date | Refund |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Unopened, in original packaging | 30 days | Full refund |
| Opened but not installed | 21 days | Refund less a 10% restocking fee |
| Installed or used | Not returnable. See section 4 for faults | None |

## 3. Items that cannot be returned

- Electrical components (thermostats, heating elements, control boards, relays) once the sealed packaging has been opened.
- Parts cut or made to order.
- Clearance items marked "final sale" on the invoice.

## 4. Warranty

- Parts are covered for 12 months from delivery against manufacturing defects. A faulty part under warranty is replaced with a new part. Warranty claims are not refunded.
- When a Quillmoor engineer installed the part, labour to replace it is also covered for 90 days from installation. After 90 days, labour is charged at the standard rate.
- Warranty does not cover damage from incorrect installation by others, misuse, or lack of the maintenance set out in the manufacturer's instructions.

## 5. How to return an item

1. Contact the service desk for a return authorization (RA) number.
2. Return the item to either depot within 10 days of receiving the RA number, with the RA number on the package.
3. Refunds are made to the original payment method within 10 business days of the item being received and inspected.

## 6. Questions

Contact the service desk. Phone lines are open 8:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday.

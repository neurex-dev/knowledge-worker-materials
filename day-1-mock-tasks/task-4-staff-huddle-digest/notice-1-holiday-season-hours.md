# Notice: Holiday Season Hours and Shipping Cut-offs

**From:** Mara Ellison, Operations Manager
**To:** All staff, North and South depots
**Date:** 21 October 2026

As in previous years, we are extending Saturday opening and moving the same-day shipping cut-off earlier to cope with the seasonal rush.

**Same-day shipping cut-off.** From Monday 16 November to Thursday 24 December, orders must be picked and staged by **14:00** for same-day dispatch. The normal cut-off is 15:00.

**Saturday opening.** North depot opens 9:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays 21 and 28 November and 5 and 12 December. Staff on the Saturday rota will receive their shifts from their supervisor by 6 November.

**Remembrance Day.** Both depots close on Wednesday 11 November.

**Holiday closure.** Both depots close from 25 December and reopen on Monday 4 January.

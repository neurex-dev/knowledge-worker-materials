# Systems Notice: Return Authorizations Move to the Service Desk System

**From:** Business Systems
**To:** Service desk, counter staff and goods-in teams
**Date:** 23 October 2026

From **Monday 2 November**, return authorization (RA) numbers will be created in the service desk system instead of the old returns spreadsheet.

**What changes for you**

- RA numbers change format, from `RA-12345` to `RT-2026-00001`.
- **Goods-in:** scan the RT number on the package. Hand-written RA numbers will no longer be accepted for new returns.
- **Counter staff:** look up the return in the service desk system. The returns spreadsheet will no longer be updated.

**Returns already in progress**

Old-format RA numbers issued before 2 November will be accepted until **30 November**. After that, the customer will need a new RT number.

**Training**

A 10-minute training video is available on the intranet. Please watch it before 2 November.

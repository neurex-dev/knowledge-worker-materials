# Task 4: Staff Huddle Digest

## The request

> Every Monday at 7:30 I run a five-minute huddle with the floor team. Head office sends a pile of notices during the week, and nobody reads them. I need a digest I can read out in five minutes: what's changing, when, and what each person has to do differently. Plain words, no jargon, and nothing they don't need.
>
> Sam Okafor, Warehouse Supervisor, North depot

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **How often** | Weekly, every Monday |
| **Time by hand** | About 30 minutes |
| **Who reads it** | Sam reads it aloud to about 15 warehouse and counter staff |

## Files

| File | What it is |
|:---|:---|
| `notice-1-holiday-season-hours.md` | Head office notice on opening hours and shipping cut-offs |
| `notice-2-north-depot-forklift-routes.md` | Safety notice on forklift routes |
| `notice-3-return-authorizations-move.md` | Systems notice on return authorization numbers |

## Background

- **Date:** treat the huddle date as Monday 26 October 2026.
- **The audience:** pickers, drivers, counter staff and the goods-in team.

Fictional sample for workshop use. Quillmoor Supply, its people, customers, suppliers and figures are invented.

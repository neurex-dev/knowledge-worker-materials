# Safety Notice: New Forklift Routes at North Depot

**From:** Health and Safety Committee
**To:** North depot staff
**Date:** 22 October 2026

Following the near miss in August, the committee approved a new forklift traffic plan for North depot. It starts on **Monday 2 November**.

**What changes**

- Aisles 4 to 7 become **one-way** for forklifts, entering from the dock end only.
- The pedestrian crossing moves from dock 2 to **dock 3**. Use the marked crossing only.
- High-visibility vests are required for everyone past the yellow line, including visitors and drivers waiting to unload.

**Refresher briefing**

All North depot staff must attend a 15-minute briefing on **Friday 30 October at 7:30** in the goods-in area before working on the floor on 2 November. Anyone on leave that day must see their supervisor for a one-to-one briefing before their first shift.

Floor markings will be repainted over the weekend of 31 October and 1 November.
